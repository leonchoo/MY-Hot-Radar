Privacy Policy

A plain-language summary of what MY Hot Radar does — and does not — collect today, because the site is currently a static MVP demonstration.

Last updated: 28 September 2026 · MVP stage

1. Overview

MY Hot Radar is currently an MVP (Minimum Viable Product). The pages on this site are static demonstration pages created to showcase layout, design, and information architecture for a future Malaysian trending-topic radar concept.

Because of that, this policy describes what the site actually does today. We will update it as real features (such as a newsletter, comments, or advertising) are added.

2. Information We Collect

The current MVP does not:

  • Require you to create an account
  • Ask you to log in
  • Collect passwords
  • Collect payment information (there is nothing to pay for)
  • Collect precise location data
  • Read your device contacts
  • Track browsing history across other sites

The information your browser automatically sends to any web server (such as IP address and user-agent) is logged by our hosting provider as part of normal web traffic, in the same way it is for any website you visit.

3. Cookies and local storage

MY Hot Radar does not currently set its own tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or analytics cookies. The MVP does not embed any first-party analytics script.

Third-party services may introduce their own technologies when they are added to the site in future. When that happens, this policy will be updated and the site will request consent where required by applicable law.

4. Advertising

The current MVP contains advertising placeholders only. No advertising network (including Google AdSense) is currently integrated into these sample pages. The placeholder rectangles marked "Advertisement" are reserved positions that may later host real ads; they do not currently deliver any advertising.

5. External links

Future versions of MY Hot Radar may link to third-party websites, social platforms, or news outlets. Once such links exist, clicking them will take you to a site that has its own privacy practices. We do not control those sites and this policy does not apply to them.

6. Data security

Because the MVP does not collect personal data through forms, accounts, or tracking, there is no user database to protect. The site is served as static files, which limits the surface area for data exposure. If real features are added in future, security controls will be put in place before any personal data is collected.

7. Changes to this policy

As MY Hot Radar evolves beyond MVP and adds features such as accounts, comments, newsletters, or advertising, this policy will be updated. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.

8. Contact

Contact details will be published when the service officially launches. Until then, please refer to the About page for context on the current stage of the project.

MY Hot Radar is currently in MVP stage. All displayed stories are sample content for demonstration purposes.
