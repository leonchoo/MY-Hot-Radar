# MY Hot Radar — Content Rules

> Voice, label, and status rules for any content published under the
> MY Hot Radar brand — including the website, Facebook Page, future
> Instagram, TikTok, X, and any other channel.

---

## Languages

| Order | Language | Use |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **简体中文** | Primary. |
| 2 | **English** | Allowed mixed, especially for brand name, tags, quoted source titles. |
| 3 | **Bahasa Malaysia** | Allowed for source quotes. Do not translate Malay-language source titles. |

Mixing is part of the brand voice. Do not separate channels by language:
a single post may include all three.

---

## Tone

- **Concise.** One core observation per paragraph. If a paragraph is doing
  two jobs, split it.
- **Natural.** Write like a friend explaining what they saw, not like a
  bulletin. Do not write like a press release.
- **Media-grade.** Acknowledge uncertainty openly. Credit source.
  Distinguish observation from claim.
- **Not sensationalized.** See "Banned phrases" below.

---

## Banned phrases (no public data, no use)

These phrases are **banned** unless accompanied by a citable, public data
point. Empty hype is not allowed.

- 全网炸锅
- 全网炸了
- 全马哀悼
- 全马疯传
- 全网崩溃
- 全网都在讨论
- 全网疯传
- 惊呆
- 震惊
- 刷爆朋友圈
- 爆了 (used as a verb with no further evidence)
- "X 万人 / 千万次 / 百万 / 刷爆 / 屠版" — if no public number exists.

If you DO have a public figure attached, you may quote it **with
attribution**. Example that IS acceptable:

> "该视频在 TikTok 上的公开数据显示，截至 [date] 已有 [N] 次观看。"

If you do not have such data, default to neutral phrasing:

> "该视频在 TikTok 上有较高浏览量。"

## Status labels (used on site and social)

The Radar's five native states are described in `NEWS_RADAR.md`. The
website may expose a subset; social posts may use one or two states in a
sentence, never as decoration.

| Label | Used when |
|---|---|
| 🔥 HOT | Already broad cross-platform presence. |
| 📈 RISING | Heat observably increasing. |
| 🐢 COOLING | Spread decelerating. |
| 🟡 WATCH | Potential; signals insufficient. |
| 🚨 BREAKING | Just emerged AND cross-source signal within window. |

**Strict rule**: a label MUST reflect propagation evidence, not the
importance of the underlying event. A government minister's tweet is
not automatically `HOT`. A pile-up near KL is not automatically `BREAKING`.

When in doubt, use `WATCH` rather than overclaim.

---

## Fact-status labels (used in body copy when reporting)

These are separate from the momentum labels above. They describe the
**epistemic state** of the report, not its heat.

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ✅ CONFIRMED | Multiple independent reliable sources; or one official source + independent corroboration. |
| 🟡 REPORTED | Outlets have published; details still need confirmation. |
| 🟠 SOCIAL BUZZ | Spread signals strong; facts not yet confirmed. |
| ❌ UNVERIFIED | Currently no reliable evidence. |
| ⚠️ RUMOUR | Known to be a rumour; do not present as fact. |

### "Single-source is not CONFIRMED" rule (binding)

- A single Facebook post ≠ CONFIRMED
- A single TikTok video ≠ CONFIRMED
- A single X post ≠ CONFIRMED
- Multi-platform presence alone ≠ CONFIRMED
- Multi-site presence that traces back to the same single wire item ≠
  multi-source confirmation

A claim is CONFIRMED only after documented cross-verification under
`VERIFICATION_RULES.md`.

## Hashtags

Each post should use **2–5** hashtags.

Composition guideline:
- 1 brand hashtag (`#MY热点雷达` or `#MYHotRadar`)
- 1–2 category hashtags (`#大马热点`, `#大马娱乐`, `#大马美食`, etc.)
- 0–1 location hashtag (`#马来西亚`)

Placement guidance:
- Either inline in the prose (more natural; less spammy) OR at the end
  on its own line.
- Do not scatter hashtags per word.

Avoid:
- `#breaking` / `#viral` / `#xxx` as a hashtag unless genuinely accurate
- Hashtags that are not readable in Simplified Chinese.

---

## Emoji policy

Emoji is allowed and used to label status. But:

- Maximum 1–3 emoji per paragraph.
- Do not end a post with a row of emoji.
- Do not use emoji as the only emphasis mechanism.
- Decorative emoji are fine in moderation.

---

## Politics (binding)

For any political topic — candidates, parties, elections, government
decisions, court decisions on political matters — apply the following:

- **Factual only.** Who said what, when, where, in which source.
- **No predictions.** No election predictions, no projected vote-share,
  no betting-style framing.
- **No endorsement or opposition.** No "support X", no "oppose Y".
  No candidate ranking. No party ranking.
- **Multi-source before CONFIRMED.** Use the fact-status labels.
- **Time-bounded.** Anchor every report to "截至 [date] [time]" so the
  post ages honestly.
- **Closing line.** Every political post ends with the equivalent of:

  > 本篇仅基于公开来源整理事实，不代表本平台立场。

## Demo / sample content (binding)

Any content that represents the MVP demo state must visibly mark itself.
Acceptable markers: `SAMPLE`, `Demo`, `MVP`, `MVP demo`.

- Do not remove the marker to make demo content look like a real post.
- Do not write a real-sounding headline about a sample topic.
- Demo articles do not get a fake byline. The byline reads
  "MY Hot Radar (demo)" or similar.

## What is NOT content yet

Anything tied to the future Radar pipeline (live data, real-time status,
candidate scoring) is **not** yet production content. See
`PROJECT_CONTEXT.md → Future Direction`.

Until the Radar is built:
- Web articles are demo / sample.
- Facebook posts may be brand-introduction or non-news commentary.
- No auto-posting of any kind.

### 中文写作风格：大白话（核心要求）

这是**必须遵守的核心写作要求**。

- 文章必须让没有专业背景的普通读者容易理解。
- 使用自然、简单、日常的中文。
- 像一个人向读者解释事情一样写，不要像政府公告、论文或商业报告。
- 先说发生了什么，再解释为什么发生。
- 尽量使用简短句子，一段讲清楚一个重点。
- 遇到专业词汇，第一次出现时用普通人听得懂的话解释。
- 可以用日常生活中的例子帮助读者理解复杂问题。
- 避免官腔、空话、复杂长句、过度专业的词汇和故意显得高深的表达。
- 不要为了吸引点击而夸大事实或制造戏剧效果。
- 如果文章看起来专业，却让普通人难以理解，就必须继续改写。

**写作原则：事实要严谨，语言要简单；事情可以复杂，解释不能复杂。**

### 文章可以使用时间线叙述

国际新闻可以采用故事式叙述，让读者理解事情的前因后果。根据事件需要，文章可以包含：

- 新闻导语：先讲清楚发生了什么。
- 事情起因：为什么会发生。
- 事件时间线：按顺序讲清楚重要节点。
- 关键变化：谁做了什么，事情如何发展。
- 各方回应：不同国家、机构或当事人分别怎么说。
- 背景解释：补充理解这件事所需的历史或政策背景。
- 对普通人和马来西亚的意义：有可靠依据时，解释实际影响。
- 目前进展：哪些已经确定，哪些还没有答案。

不要求每篇文章都使用全部章节。根据新闻内容自然安排，避免机械套模板。
时间线中每个重要节点必须有可靠来源支持，不能为了让故事完整而虚构细节。

### 必须提供独特价值

- 不能仅仅翻译、替换同义词、拼接来源文章，或为了增加文章数量而批量生成内容。
- 改写后应尽可能增加实质性价值，例如：
  - 用简单中文解释复杂事件。
  - 把分散的可靠信息整理成清楚的时间线。
  - 补充有来源支持的背景。
  - 解释不同说法之间的区别。
  - 说明事件对普通读者或马来西亚的实际意义。
  - 区分已知事实与仍待确认的问题。
- 如果无法提供足够的独特价值，就暂缓发布，不要为了凑数量而写稿。
